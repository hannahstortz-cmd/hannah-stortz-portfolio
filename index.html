A Little About Me

Currently, I am a senior web design student who loves using research to understand a problem and creating designs that address specific user needs. From a young age, I had a deep passion for art, which led me to begin taking college classes on graphic design in high school. During my junior year of high school, I took an HTML/CSS class. That experience sparked my interest in web design, and I began teaching myself React and Figma.

Recently, I worked on a redesign project for a church near my hometown. Through user testing, surveys, and other research methods, I sought to understand why the navigation and page structure were confusing. Ultimately, I redesigned both the structure and aesthetics of the site, and follow-up user testing showed a 75% average reduction in task completion time. I’d love to connect!
